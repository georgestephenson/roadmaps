# How the web works

## Search engine optimisation (SEO)

[Google's guide here](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)

- Use descriptive URLs
- Group similar pages in directories
- Reduce duplicate content
- Keep content: unique, easy to read, up to date, helpful
- <title> elements may be used for Google search title
- Add high-quality, optimised images, with alt text
- Promote the website, get links on other sites referring to it (but going too far risks being penalised for manipulating search results)
- Make links and page content all crawlable so Google search crawler can navigate everything

## Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)

- Sources say "GEO is the new SEO" - getting your site to appear in AI-generated answers
- SEO fundamentals are basically still very important (site performance, number of links across web)
- Use do extensive marketing so your site is mentioned across the internet
